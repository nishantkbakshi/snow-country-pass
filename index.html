Snow country manners

Small things that make hosts smile, and make you feel at home.

At the onsen

  • ✓Wash and rinse fully at the showers before you get in
  • ✓Bathe without swimwear; keep the small towel out of the water
  • ✕No photos in the bathing area
  • ✓Have a tattoo? Choose a private bath; your pass can arrange one

Sake and the table

  • ✓Pour for others, and let them pour for you
  • ✓Say "itadakimasu" before eating; "gochisousama" after
  • ✕Do not stand chopsticks upright in rice, or pass food chopstick to chopstick
  • ✓Do not drink? Every tasting has a rice or tea version

At the inn, in town, on the train

  • ✓Shoes off at the ryokan entrance; slippers inside, none on tatami
  • ✓Keep your voice low and phone on silent on trains
  • ✕No tipping; good service is simply expected
  • ✓Carry your rubbish with you; public bins are rare
